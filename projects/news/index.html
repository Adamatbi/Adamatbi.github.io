Vancouver Hammock Map: On the CBC



I recently had the pleasure of being invited onto the CBC to join Stephen Quinn on The Early Edition. The interview was about a recent project using city-level mapping data to find every location to hang a hammock in the city of Vancouver. The project was a bit of a laugh, but the response it received was overwhelming. It was an interesting experience being featured on the CBC and online.

If you're interested in hearing the interview, you can find it here.
